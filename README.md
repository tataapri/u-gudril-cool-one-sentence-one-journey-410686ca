A complete story about a journey told in exactly one sentence, written by main.ts to /output/entry.txt.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

This entry tells the story of Mara, who boards a night train carrying only her grandmother's brass compass and a ticket to a town she cannot pronounce, and arrives at dawn a stranger to herself, following the compass home to a door she has never seen. The whole arc — departure, night crossing, and an unexpected arrival — lives inside a single sentence of under 300 characters, with no title and no second sentence. Running `node main.ts` writes that sentence, exactly and deterministically with no trailing newline, to `/output/entry.txt` (or to the path in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT` when set).
